Say & Share — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Say & Share is published by Dice in a Box Software. This policy explains how the current Windows and Android versions handle information when you use the app. It does not describe a future web version or other features that have not been released.

Information you enter

Say & Share processes the words, letters and phrases you select or type so that it can display your message and, when requested, speak it aloud. You may choose to enter names or other personal information. We do not require you to provide a name, email address or account to use the app.

The current message is held in the app's working memory. Say & Share does not intentionally save a conversation or message history. Clear removes the current message from the interface and working message buffer; it does not delete saved custom phrases, dictionary words, speech-provider records or operating-system backups.

Information saved on your device

The app saves custom button words and phrases, words you add to the spelling dictionary, and preferences such as appearance, layout, spelling region, predictive dimming, Speak as you go and whether you have opened the instructions. These are used to personalise the app and remember your choices between sessions.

This information is stored in the app's data files on your device. The app also keeps local recovery copies of its settings and custom dictionary to protect against damaged or interrupted saves. It does not apply its own password protection or encryption to these files. Device security, user-account permissions and any device encryption help protect access.

Speaking your messages

When you choose Speak or Done / Speak, the text is passed to the text-to-speech service available on your device. When Speak as you go is enabled, individual words or letters are also passed to that service as you add them. The Android app may send a fixed, non-personal word at zero volume during startup to prepare the speech engine.

Speech processing is performed by your operating system or installed speech provider, not by a Dice in a Box Software speech server. Some voices operate on the device; others may require an internet connection and process text through the provider's online services. This depends on your installed engine, voice and device settings. The provider's own privacy policy applies to its processing. Check those settings and policies before speaking sensitive information. Spoken messages can also be heard by people nearby.

Network services, advertising and tracking

The current app code does not upload your messages, custom phrases or dictionary to Dice in a Box Software. It does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or an automatic crash-reporting service operated by us. Spelling predictions use bundled word lists and your saved dictionary rather than an online generative-AI service.

Your operating system, speech provider and app store may separately process information for services such as speech, purchases, updates, diagnostics and backups under their own privacy policies. We do not promise that the entire device or all speech providers operate offline.

Purchases and support

Where you purchase the app through an app store, that store handles payment and account information under its own policies. Say & Share does not ask you to enter payment-card information inside the app.

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device details, screenshots and a description of the problem. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain support records. Please avoid sending private messages, medical information or other sensitive material unless it is needed to explain the issue. Support messages are also processed by the email or support service used to deliver them.

Retention and deletion

Saved preferences, custom phrases and dictionary words remain on your device until you change or remove them or remove the app's stored data. You can edit or clear custom buttons and remove added words using the app's controls. Earlier values may remain in a local recovery copy until it is replaced by a later save.

For a fuller local reset, remove the app's data, including recovery files, using the options available on your device. Uninstalling may not remove all data on every platform. Device or cloud backups may retain copies independently; manage those through the relevant backup service. Dice in a Box Software cannot remotely read or erase your device's local app files.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant support records or meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have sent directly to us; the rights available depend on applicable law.

Children and supported users

Say & Share may be used with help from a parent, carer or other support person. The app does not require a child to register an account or submit personal information to us. A parent or support person should help choose appropriate custom content, review speech-provider settings and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in support requests.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms to this page.
